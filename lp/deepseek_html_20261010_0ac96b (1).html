🐛 Hi, I'm an Isopod Hobbyist
Tiny woodlice, big world
Welcome to LittleIsopods! I'm an amateur enthusiast obsessed with isopods (woodlice). It all started when I found a little pill bug curled up under a flowerpot — I was instantly captivated by these charming little crustaceans. They may be small, but they're incredible decomposers in nature. Here I share the species I keep, my observations, and hope to connect with fellow hobbyists. 🐚✨
